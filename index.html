Traditions & Celebrations

The meaningful rituals and moments that guide our journey

The Sacred Muhurtham

The Auspicious Lagna • Thursday, 10th December 2026 • Ranebennur

Held in the auspicious Dhanu Lagna, the Muhurtham is the heart of the wedding ceremony at Bellary Kalyana Mantapa[cite: 17]. Amid Vedic chants and the sacred fire, the couple partakes in time-honored rituals—including Kanyadaana and the exchange of sacred vows—symbolizing the coming together of two families with honor, respect, and mutual trust.

Mangalya Dharana & Akshata

The Tie of Unity & Blessings • Thursday, 10th December 2026 • Ranebennur

Following the vows in Ranebennur, the tying of the sacred Mangalsutra marks the eternal union of the couple as lifelong partners. Family and guests shower them with Akshata (consecrated unbroken rice infused with turmeric), bestowing prayers for prosperity, long life, and unbroken togetherness before gathering for the traditional wedding lunch.

The Evening Soiree

Celebration Under the Stars • Saturday, 19th December 2026 • Bengaluru

Following the traditional ceremonies in Ranebennur, we gather amidst the open lawns of Holiday Village Resort on Kanakapura Road[cite: 17]. An evening dedicated to celebration, heartfelt conversations, and dinner under the Bengaluru night sky with our dearest family and friends.
